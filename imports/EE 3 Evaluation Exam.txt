1. A two terminal pair of Network of a transmission line can be represented by a _____.
A. T- Network
B. π - Network
C. Either A or B
D. tree network
Answer: C
Solution: A transmission line is a two-port (two terminal-pair) network and can be represented either by a nominal-T or a nominal-π equivalent circuit.

2. How many hours can a load be called a Continuous load?
A. One hour
B. 3 hours
C. 2 hours
D. 24 hours
Answer: B
Solution: PEC definition: a continuous load is one where the maximum current is expected to continue for 3 hours or more.

3. A lamp give sufficient light to an object at a distance of 70 cm. By how much closer should the object be placed to double the illumination.
A. 21.55 cm
B. 22.45 cm
C. 20.5 cm
D. 21.28 cm
Answer: C
Solution: E ∝ 1/d² (inverse square law). For 2E: d2 = d1/√2 = 70/1.4142 = 49.5 cm
• Move closer by 70 − 49.5 = 20.5 cm

4. Determine the per unit impedance of a 3-phase, 60 MVA, 13.2 kV Generator with 20% impedance at 100 MVA and 13.2 kV bases.
A. 0.54
B. 0.12
C. 0.28
D. 0.33
Answer: D
Solution: Same kV base, so Z(new) = Z(old) × (MVA new/MVA old)
• Z = 0.20 × 100/60 = 0.333 pu

5. The growth rate of the energy consumption in our country is 6.9%. In how many years will the energy be quadrupled?
A. 20
B. 30
C. 15
D. 25
Answer: A
Solution: Continuous growth: E = E₀e^(rt), so 4 = e^(0.069t)
• t = ln 4/0.069 = 1.3863/0.069 = 20.1 ≈ 20 years

Solution: Coal (a fossil fuel) is the most conventional energy source; solar, geothermal and wind are non-conventional (renewable) sources.

13. Which of the following is incorrect about HVDC.

16. The effect of series capacitor on a transmission line is to improve the.
A. steady state stability
B. transient stability
C. both A and B
D. none of the above
Answer: C
Solution: Series capacitors reduce the net series reactance X. Since Pmax = VsVr/X, both steady-state and transient stability limits are improved.

18. The power transmission capacity of the transmission line is.

19. A power plant consumes 3600 short tons of coal per day. If the coal has an average energy content of 10,000 btu/lb. Assume an overall efficiency of 15%, what is the plant’s output MW?
A. 132
B. 235
C. 292
D. 86
Answer: A
Solution: Coal per day = 3600 × 2000 = 7.2 × 10⁶ lb; heat = 7.2 × 10¹⁰ BTU/day

20. A three-phase circuit, 60 Hz, 345 kV transposed transmission line is composed of 1272 MCM, 54/19 conductors per phase with horizontal spacing of 35 ft, 35 ft. and 70 ft respectively. If the GMR of the ACSR conductors is 14.2mm, find the inductance in mH/km per phase of the transmission line?
A. 1.37
B. 1.98
C. 1.28
D. 1.64
Answer: A
Solution: GMD = ∛(35 × 35 × 70) = 44.1 ft = 13.44 m; GMR = 0.0142 m
• L = 2 × 10⁻⁷ ln(13.44/0.0142) = 2 × 10⁻⁷ × 6.853 = 1.371 × 10⁻⁶ H/m = 1.37 mH/km

21. The effective resistance of conductor is increased by
A. skin effect
B. proximity effect
C. corona effect
D. both A and B
Answer: D
Solution: Skin effect and proximity effect both crowd the current into part of the conductor cross-section, increasing its effective AC resistance. Corona causes a power loss, not a resistance increase.

22. Which of the folllowing insulator will be selected for high voltage application?
A. strain type
B. suspension type
C. disc type
D. pin type
Answer: B
Solution: Pin insulators are economical only up to about 33 kV; for higher voltages suspension-type insulators (strings of discs) are used.

23. If the Transmission line voltage is doubled with the same load, the line loss is _________
A. 2 times
B. 4 times
C. ¼ times
D. ½ times
Answer: C
Solution: For the same power, I ∝ 1/V, so loss I²R ∝ 1/V². Doubling V gives (1/2)² = ¼ the loss.

24. A small wind generator is to be designed to generate 75 KW of power at a wind velocity of 48 kph. What is the approximate blade diameter?
A. 26 ft
B. 42 ft
C. 36 ft
D. 34 ft
Answer: D
Solution: v = 48 kph = 13.33 m/s; wind power density = ½ρv³ = 0.5 × 1.225 × 13.33³ = 1452 W/m²
• At the Betz limit (Cp = 0.593): 0.593 × 1452 = 861 W/m², so A = 75,000/861 = 87.1 m²
• D = √(4A/π) = 10.5 m = 34.6 ft ≈ 34 ft
• Review note: if the power coefficient is ignored (100% of wind power), D ≈ 26.7 ft (choice A). Confirm which assumption your key uses.

Answer: D
Solution: Overhead ground (shield) wires above the phase conductors intercept direct lightning strokes and are the most effective protection for transmission lines.

30. Calculate the capacitance per phase to neutral per km of a single circuit, two bundled conductor line. The diameter of each conductor is 5 cm, spacing of conductors of the bundle is 30 cm and horizontal spacing between adjacent phases are 5 m.

B. 0.0132 μF/km
