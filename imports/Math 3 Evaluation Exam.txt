Math 3 Evaluation Exam
Coverage: Differential Equations, Advanced Eng'g. Mathematics, Permutation, Combination and Probability

1. Infinity minus infinity is:
A. infinity
B. indeterminate
C. zero
D. none of these
Answer: B
Solution: ∞ − ∞ is an indeterminate form: its value depends on how the two quantities grow (e.g. (x + 1) − x → 1 but 2x − x → ∞).

2. Two or more equations are equal if and only if they have the same:
A. solution set
B. order
C. degree
D. variable set
Answer: A
Solution: Equations are equivalent (equal) exactly when every solution of one is a solution of the other, i.e. they have the same solution set.

3. Simplify (3 − i)² − 7(3 − i) + 10.
A. −(3 + i)
B. 3 + i
C. 3 − i
D. −(3 − i)
Answer: D
Solution: Let w = 3 − i. Then w² − 7w + 10 = (w − 2)(w − 5) = (1 − i)(−2 − i)
• = −2 − i + 2i + i² = −2 + i − 1 = −3 + i = −(3 − i)

4. If seven coins are tossed simultaneously, find the probability that there will be at least five heads.
A. 29/128
B. 1/2
C. 5/128
D. 37/128
Answer: A
Solution: P(at least 5 heads) = [C(7,5) + C(7,6) + C(7,7)]/2⁷ = (21 + 7 + 1)/128 = 29/128
• Review note: the PDF reads "at five heads"; exactly five heads would be 21/128, which is not a choice, so "at least" is meant.

5. A die is rolled and a coin is tossed. What is the probability that a three and a head will appear?
A. 1/2
B. 2/3
C. 1/6
D. 1/12
Answer: D
Solution: Independent events: P = (1/6)(1/2) = 1/12
• Review note: the PDF prints choices A and C both as 1/2; C is shown here as 1/6 (a likely misprint). The answer is D either way.

6. The quartile deviation is a measure of:
A. division
B. certainty
C. central tendency
D. dispersion
Answer: D
Solution: The quartile deviation (semi-interquartile range), (Q₃ − Q₁)/2, measures the spread of the data, i.e. dispersion.
• Review note: the PDF reads "quartile division"; "quartile deviation" is meant.

9. Two factors are considered essentially the same if:
A. one is merely the negative of the other
B. both of them are negative
C. one is exactly the same as the other
D. both of them are positive
Answer: A
Solution: In factoring, factors that differ only by a unit (such as −1) are regarded as essentially the same, e.g. (x − y) and (y − x).

13. In the chemical processing of a certain mineral, the rate of change of the amount of mineral present varies as the amount of the mineral remaining. If after 8 hours, 100 pounds of mineral have been reduced to 70 pounds, what quantity of the mineral will remain after 24 hours?
A. 53.2
B. 34.3
C. 43.4
D. 25.3
Answer: B
Solution: dA/dt = kA gives A = 100e^(kt); every 8 hours the amount is multiplied by 0.70.
• A(24) = 100(0.70)^(24/8) = 100(0.343) = 34.3 lb

14. You are given n = 10 measurements: 3, 5, 4, 6, 10, 5, 6, 9, 2, 8. Find the mode.
A. 8
B. 5
C. 6
D. 5 & 6
Answer: D
Solution: 5 and 6 each occur twice; every other value occurs once. The data are bimodal: modes 5 and 6.

15. Find the orthogonal trajectories of the family of curves with center at the origin and foci on the x-axis.
A. y = Cx²
B. y = C ln(x/y)
C. y = Cx^(b²/a²)
D. y = Cx^((a/b)²)
Answer: D
Solution: Family: x²/a² + y²/b² = c, i.e. b²x² + a²y² = k.
• Differentiate: b²x + a²yy' = 0, so y' = −b²x/(a²y)
• Orthogonal trajectories: y' = a²y/(b²x), so dy/y = (a²/b²)dx/x
• ln y = (a²/b²) ln x + ln C, so y = Cx^(a²/b²) = Cx^((a/b)²)

16. A statement the truth of which is admitted without proof is called:
A. an axiom
B. a theorem
C. a postulate
D. a corollary
Answer: A
Solution: An axiom is a statement accepted as true without proof. A theorem is proved; a corollary follows directly from a theorem.
• Review note: "postulate" is often used with nearly the same meaning (traditionally for geometric assumptions); the usual key is "axiom".

17. In any square matrix, when the elements of any two rows/columns are interchanged, the determinant is ___.
A. negative of former value
B. the same
C. unity
D. zero
Answer: A
Solution: Interchanging two rows (or two columns) of a determinant changes its sign.

22. If the lengths 2, 3, 5, 7 and 9 are to be used to form a triangle, what is the probability that a triangle is formed?
A. 7/10
B. 3/10
C. 4/5
D. 3/5
Answer: B
Solution: There are C(5,3) = 10 sets of three lengths. A triangle needs the two shorter sides to sum to more than the longest.
• Valid: (3, 5, 7), (3, 7, 9), (5, 7, 9) — 3 sets. (2, 3, 5), (2, 5, 7) and (2, 7, 9) only reach equality, so they fail.
• P = 3/10

32. The cost of renting a ballroom for a school dance is different at each of three establishments. The Ambassador charges $100 plus $3 per person. The Belmont charges $150 plus $2 per person, while the Coliseum charges $250 plus $1 per person. Under what condition would the Belmont be the place to hold the dance?
A. Less than 30
B. between 30-50
C. between 50-75
D. between 50-100
Answer: D
Solution: For n persons: Ambassador 100 + 3n, Belmont 150 + 2n, Coliseum 250 + n.

• Belmont < Coliseum: 150 + 2n < 250 + n, so n < 100
• Belmont is cheapest for 50 < n < 100

33. Find the dimensions of the right circular cylinder of greatest volume that can be inscribed in a right circular cone of radius r and altitude h.
A. radius: 2/3 r ; altitude: 2/3 h
B. radius: 2/3 r ; altitude: 1/3 h
C. radius: 1/3 r ; altitude: 1/3 h
D. radius: 1/3 r ; altitude: 2/3 h
Answer: B
