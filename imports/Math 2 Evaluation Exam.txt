1. Two houses A and B are to be connected to a water main 1200 m long and it is desired to tap the main in one place only. If A and B are 300 m and 900 m from the main pipe respectively, where must the pipe be tapped to use the least pipe?
A. 300 m
B. 400 m
C. 500 m
D. 600 m
Answer: A
Solution: Let the tap be x m from the foot of A's perpendicular. Pipe length L = √(x² + 300²) + √((1200 − x)² + 900²).
• dL/dx = 0 gives x/√(x² + 300²) = (1200 − x)/√((1200 − x)² + 900²), i.e. similar triangles: x/300 = (1200 − x)/900
• 900x = 360,000 − 300x, so x = 300 m (from A's end of the main)

2. Find the area enclosed by the curve r² = 4 cos θ.
A. 4
B. 8
C. 16
D. 2
Answer: B
Solution: r² = 4 cos θ is real for −π/2 ≤ θ ≤ π/2. r = +√(4 cos θ) traces one loop and r = −√(4 cos θ) traces its mirror loop.
• One loop: A₁ = ½∫ from −π/2 to π/2 of 4 cos θ dθ = ½(8) = 4
• Both loops: A = 2(4) = 8 square units
• Review note: if the curve was meant to be the lemniscate r² = 4 cos 2θ, the area would be 4 (choice A).

3. A ladder 10 ft long rests against a vertical wall. If the foot of the ladder slips away from the wall at a rate of 2 ft/min, how fast is the angle between the ladder and the building changing when the foot of the ladder is 6 ft away from the building?
A. 1/2 rad/min
B. 1/4 rad/min
C. 18 rad/min
D. 1 rad/min
Answer: B
Solution: Let θ be the angle between the ladder and the wall: sin θ = x/10.
• cos θ (dθ/dt) = (1/10)(dx/dt)
• At x = 6 the height is 8, so cos θ = 8/10
• dθ/dt = 2/(10 × 0.8) = 1/4 rad/min

4. A mercury light hangs 12 ft above the island at the center of Ayala Avenue, which is 24 ft wide. A cigarette vendor 5 ft tall walks along the curb of the street at a speed of 420 fpm. How fast is the shadow lengthening when he is 16 ft up the street?
A. 8 fps
B. 4 fps
C. 6 fps
D. 10 fps
Answer: B
Solution: The curb is 12 ft from the point under the light. Let y = distance walked along the curb from the nearest point, D = √(12² + y²) = ground distance from the foot of the light.
• Similar triangles: s/5 = (D + s)/12, so s = 5D/7
• At y = 16: D = √(144 + 256) = 20 ft; dy/dt = 420 fpm = 7 fps
• dD/dt = (y/D)(dy/dt) = (16/20)(7) = 5.6 fps
• ds/dt = (5/7)(5.6) = 4 fps

5. Find a point of inflection for the curve y = 3x⁴ − 8x³ + 6x².
A. (1/4, 1/2)
B. (1/2, 11/24)
C. (1/5, 1/8)
D. (1/3, 11/27)
Answer: D
Solution: y' = 12x³ − 24x² + 12x; y'' = 36x² − 48x + 12 = 12(3x − 1)(x − 1)
• y'' = 0 at x = 1/3 and x = 1 (y'' changes sign at both)
• At x = 1/3: y = 3/81 − 8/27 + 6/9 = 1/27 − 8/27 + 18/27 = 11/27
• Point of inflection: (1/3, 11/27)

13. Suppose you make napkin rings by drilling holes with different diameters through two wooden balls (which also have different diameters). 17. In conic sections, if the discriminant B² − 4AC = 1, the locus is a:
A. circle
B. ellipse
C. parabola
D. hyperbola
Answer: D
Solution: B² − 4AC > 0 → hyperbola; = 0 → parabola; < 0 → ellipse (circle when B = 0 and A = C). Here it equals 1 > 0, so hyperbola.

18. At maximum values, the first derivative of a function is _____, while at minimum values the first derivative is _____.
A. zero, also zero
B. positive, negative
C. negative, positive
D. zero, positive
Answer: A
Solution: At both a maximum and a minimum of a smooth function, the tangent is horizontal, so the first derivative is zero at both.

21. Two legs of a right triangle are each 70 cm. If one leg grows at the rate of 5 cm/min and the other shrinks at the rate of 5 cm/min, how fast is the hypotenuse of the triangle changing 2 minutes later?

22. A television camera is positioned 5000 ft from the base of a rocket launching pad. The camera is designed to follow the vertical path of the rocket. If the rocket's speed is 500 ft/sec when it has risen 2000 ft, how fast is the camera's angle of elevation changing at this instant?

35. It costs a bus company P125 to run a bus on a certain tour, plus P15 per passenger. The capacity of the bus is 20 persons and the company charges P35 per ticket if the bus is full. C. 15
D. 8
Answer: A
