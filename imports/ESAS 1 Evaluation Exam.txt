1. A collision in which the total kinetic energy after collision is less than before collision is?
A. Inelastic Collision
B. Elastic Collision
C. Perfectly Elastic Collision
D. Out of Center Collision
Answer: A
Solution: In an inelastic collision momentum is conserved but some kinetic energy is lost (to heat, sound or deformation), so the kinetic energy after the collision is less than before.

2. It is a branch of physics which concerned with the study of forces and torques and their effect on motion.
A. Dynamics
B. Kinematics
C. Kinetic Energy
D. Continuum
Answer: A
Solution: Dynamics studies forces and torques and the motion they produce. Kinematics describes motion without regard to its causes.

3. A vertically loaded beam, fixed at one end and simply supported at the other is indeterminate to what degree?
A. Third
B. First
C. Second
D. Statically Determinate
Answer: B
Solution: A propped cantilever has 4 reactions (fixed end: V and M; simple support: V; plus H) against 3 equations of equilibrium; for vertical loading it has 3 unknowns (V1, M1, V2) against 2 equations.
• Either way there is one redundant: indeterminate to the first degree.

4. A block is dragged up an inclined plane of slope angle of 20 deg. by force of F= 18.4 lbs, 30 degrees with the plane. How large will the component Fy in lbs be?
A. 14
B. 12
C. 11
D. 9.2
Answer: A
Solution: The force makes 20° + 30° = 50° with the horizontal.
• Fy (vertical) = 18.4 sin 50° = 14.1 lb ≈ 14 lb
• Fx (horizontal) = 18.4 cos 50° = 11.8 lb
• Review note: if x and y are taken along and normal to the incline, Fy = 18.4 sin 30° = 9.2 lb (choice D). The choices 14 and 12 fit horizontal/vertical axes.

5. Two boys B1 and B2, the effort of B11 and B2 acts at a point. The effort of B1 is 8 lbs and his direction is 60 deg above horizontal on the first quadrant. The magnitude of B2 is 5lbs and his direction is 53 deg below horizontal on the fourth quadrant. What is the resultant effort of the two boys?
A. R = 8.6 lbs
B. R = 14 lbs

Answer: C

6. A man standing at a window 5 meters tall, a falling ball passes by the window 0.3 sec. from how high above the top of the window was the ball released?
A. 8.18 m
B. 9.63 m
C. 11.8 m
D. 21.3 m
Answer: C
Solution: Let v be the ball's speed at the top of the window: 5 = v(0.3) + ½(9.81)(0.3)²
• 5 = 0.3v + 0.441, so v = 15.20 m/s
• Height above the top of the window: h = v²/(2g) = 15.20²/(2 × 9.81) = 11.8 m

7. A balloon is rising uniformly with a velocity of 10fps when a stone is dropped from it. The stone then reaches the ground in 3 sec. find the height of the balloon when the stone hits the ground?
A. 145 ft
B. 148 ft
C. 156 ft
D. 180 ft
Answer: A
Solution: The stone starts with the balloon's upward velocity, 10 ft/s.
• Height at release: h₀ = −(10)(3) + ½(32.2)(3)² = −30 + 144.9 = 114.9 ft
• The balloon rises another 10 × 3 = 30 ft, so its height = 114.9 + 30 = 144.9 ≈ 145 ft

9. A ball is dropped onto a solid floor from an initial height, ho. If the coefficient of restitution, e is 0.90. How high will the ball rebound?
A. 0.45 ho
B. 0.85 ho
C. 0.81 ho
D. 0.90 ho
Answer: C
Solution: e = √(h/h₀), so h = e²h₀ = (0.90)²h₀ = 0.81h₀

10. The center of gravity of a log 10 ft long and weighing 100lbs is 4 ft from one end of the log. It is to be carried by two men, one at the heavy end. Where should the other man hold the log if each is to carry half of the load?
A. 9ft
B. 6ft
C. 4ft
D. 8ft
Answer: D
Solution: With equal loads the two supports must be equally far from the center of gravity.
• One man is at the heavy end, 4 ft from the CG, so the other must be 4 ft beyond it: 4 + 4 = 8 ft from the heavy end.

11. A block is pulled to the right at a constant velocity by a 10lb force acting 30deg above the horizontal. The coefficient of sliding friction between the block and surfaces is 0.5. What is the weight of the block? Assume all of the forces on the block to act at its center.

12. A steel ball is dropped from 2 meter height onto a smooth floor, if the coefficient of restitution between the ball and floor is 0.87, the ball will bound to height h1 near to

13. A 5-gram bullet is fired horizontally with a speed of 320 m/sec into a 1.0 kg wooden block at rest on a table. If the coefficient of friction between bock and the table is 0.25, how far will the block slide?
A. 0.515m
B. 0.615m
C. 0.315m
D. 0.715m

14. An object weighing 425 lbs is held by a rope that passes over a horizontal drum. The coefficient of friction between the rope and the drop is 0.27. If the angle of contact is 160 deg. determine the force that will raise the object.
A. 1000 lbs
B. 800 lbs
C. 700 lbs
D. 900 lbs
Answer: D
Solution: Belt friction: T₂ = T₁e^(μθ), θ = 160° = 2.793 rad
• μθ = 0.27 × 2.793 = 0.754, e^0.754 = 2.125
• T₂ = 425 × 2.125 = 903 lb ≈ 900 lb

15. A flywheel whose angular acceleration is constant and equal to 2 rads/sec square rotates through an angle of 100 radians in 5 sec. how long had it been in motion at the beginning of the 5 sec interval if it stated from rest?
A. 7.5 sec
B. 5.5 sec
C. 10.5 sec
D. 9.5 sec
Answer: A
Solution: θ = ω₀t + ½αt²: 100 = 5ω₀ + ½(2)(25), so ω₀ = 15 rad/s at the start of the interval
• From rest: t = ω₀/α = 15/2 = 7.5 s

16. A pendulum clock which keeps correct time at a point where g=980.0cm/sec² is found to lose 10 sec per day at a higher altitude. Find the value of “g” at the new location.
A. 977.78 cm/sec²
B. 980.00 cm/sec²

17. The straight line portion of the stress-strain diagram has a slope equal to the _____ of the material.
A. Shear Modulus
B. Modulus of Resilience
C. Compressibility
D. Modulus of Elasticity
Answer: D
Solution: In the linear (proportional) region σ = Eε, so the slope is the modulus of elasticity E.

18. What is the reciprocal of bulk modulus?
A. Young’s Modulus
B. Tensile Modulus
C. Compressibility
D. Shear Modulus

Solution: Compressibility β = 1/K, the fractional volume change per unit pressure.

19. What is the ratio of the stress to strain below the proportional limit?
A. Poisson Ratio
B. Young’s Modulus
C. Hooke’s Constant
D. Modulus of Rigidity
Answer: B
Solution: Below the proportional limit, stress/strain = E, Young's modulus (Hooke's law).

20. In bending of a rectangular beam due to axial loading, where is the location of maximum shear stress?
A. At the top edge
B. At the neutral axis
C. At the bottom edge
D. At the between the top edge and the neutral axis
Answer: B
Solution: τ = VQ/(Ib). Q is largest at the neutral axis and zero at the edges, so the maximum shear stress (1.5V/A for a rectangle) occurs at the neutral axis.

21. What force is required to punch a 0.75 inch circular hole through a piece of 5/8 inch thick steel plate? The ultimate shear strength is 42ksi.
A. 65,250 lbs
B. 62,850 lbs
C. 68,250 lbs
D. 61,850 lbs
Answer: D
Solution: Sheared area = πdt = π(0.75)(0.625) = 1.4726 in²

22. A steel support must connect two 30,000lb tensile loads separated by 200 inch. The maximum allowable stress is 10,000 lbf/in² and the maximum allowable elongation is 0.02 inch. What is the required area?
A. 10 in²
B. 14.1 m²
C. 56 cm²
D. 30.5 mm²
Answer: A
Solution: By stress: A = P/σ = 30,000/10,000 = 3 in²
• By elongation (E = 30 × 10⁶ psi): A = PL/(Eδ) = 30,000 × 200/(30 × 10⁶ × 0.02) = 10 in²
• The larger area governs: A = 10 in²

23. Which of the following may be the Poisson of a material?
A. 0.45
B. 0.50
C. 0.55
D. 0.85
Answer: A
Solution: For isotropic materials Poisson's ratio lies between 0 and 0.5; most engineering materials are 0.25–0.45. 0.45 is a realistic value.
• Note: 0.50 is the theoretical upper limit (a perfectly incompressible material); 0.55 and 0.85 are impossible.

24. At a yield stress of 250 MPa the corresponding strain is 0.025 mm/mm. what is its modulus of resilience?
A. 1.563 MPa
B. 6.25 MPa
C. 3.125 MPa
D. 12.5 MPa

Solution: Modulus of resilience = ½σε = ½(250)(0.025) = 3.125 MPa (MJ/m³)

25. A welded steel cylindrical drum made of a 100-mm plate has an internal diameter of 1.20 m. compute change in diameter that would be caused by an internal pressure of 1.4 MPa. Assume that Poisson’s ratio is 0.30 and E=200 GPa.
A. 0.428 mm
B. 0.549 mm
C. 1.549 mm
D. 1.495 mm
Answer: A

• ΔD = εhD = 3.57 × 10⁻⁴ × 1200 = 0.428 mm
• Review note: the PDF prints a "100-mm plate"; the choices fit a 10-mm plate (with 100 mm, ΔD = 0.043 mm).

29. What is the SI unit of pressure?

30. Kinematic viscosity may be expressed as:
A. slug-sec²/ft
B. sec²/ft
C. ft²/sec
D. slugs/sec²
Answer: C
Solution: Kinematic viscosity ν = μ/ρ, with units of area per time: ft²/s (m²/s in SI).

31. Absolute viscosity of a fluid varies with pressure and temperature and is defined as a function of ______.
A. Shear Stress and Angular Deformation Rate
B. Density, Shear Stress and Angular Deformation Rate
C. Density and Angular Deformation Rate
D. Density and Shear Stress
Answer: A
Solution: Newton's law of viscosity: τ = μ(du/dy), so μ = shear stress/rate of angular deformation. Density does not enter.

32. What is the dimensionless number that is used to describe by viscosity of fluid?
A. Reynolds Number
B. Kinematic Viscosity
C. Coefficient of Viscosity
D. Mach Number
Answer: A
Solution: The Reynolds number Re = ρVD/μ is the dimensionless ratio of inertial to viscous forces.

33. What refers to the formation and collapse of minute bubbles of vapour in liquid which caused by a combination of reduced pressure and increased velocity in the fluid?
A. Cavitation
B. Stress Corrosion
C. Fatigue Flow
D. Precipitation
Answer: A
Solution: Cavitation: where velocity rises and pressure falls below the vapour pressure, vapour bubbles form and then collapse violently.

34. What is the term which defines the combination of Laminar and turbulent flow?
A. Reynold’s Flow
B. Hydraulic Jump
C. Undefined
Answer: C
Solution: Between laminar flow (Re < 2000) and turbulent flow (Re > 4000) is the transition (critical) zone, where the flow is not clearly either; it is described as undefined (transitional) flow.

35. In the design of waterways, the “hydraulic jump” is sometimes used for _______.
A. Elimination of Turbulence
B. Energy Dissipation
C. Prevention of Sedimentation
D. Measurement of Flow
Answer: B
Solution: A hydraulic jump turns fast supercritical flow into slow subcritical flow with a large loss of energy, so it is used to dissipate energy below spillways and gates.

36. On doubling the speed, N, of the a centrifugal pump, all of the following are true, except:
A. volume flow rate, Q is increased by a factor 2
B. head, H is increased by a factor of 4
C. horsepower, P is increased by a factor of 8
D. head, horsepower and volume flow rate are independently variable
Answer: D
Solution: Affinity laws: Q ∝ N, H ∝ N², P ∝ N³. Doubling N gives 2Q, 4H and 8P, so A, B and C are true. They are tied to speed, not independently variable.

37. The static level of water in the wells penetrating the zone saturation is ________.
A. Artesian Table
B. Hydrostatic Level
C. Aquifer Level
D. Water Table
Answer: D
Solution: The water table is the upper surface of the zone of saturation, the level at which water stands in wells that penetrate it.

38. In a system of pipes in series, which is true?
A. Flow Are Equal
B. Head Losses Are Equal
C. Velocities Are Equal
D. Friction Coefficients Are Equal
Answer: A
Solution: Pipes in series carry the same discharge (continuity); the head losses add. (In parallel, head losses are equal and flows add.)

39. At or below critical velocity in small pipes or at very low velocities, the loss of head due to friction …
A. Varies Directly As Velocity
B. Can Be Ignored
C. Is Infinitely Large
D. Varies As The Velocity Squared
Answer: A
Solution: Below the critical velocity the flow is laminar: hf = 32μLV/(γD²) (Hagen–Poiseuille), so the loss varies directly with velocity.

40. The losses in open channel flow generally varies as the ________.
A. Square of velocity
B. First power of the roughness coefficient
C. Inverse square of hydraulic radius
D. Inverse of the roughness coefficient
Answer: A
Solution: Open channel flow is normally fully turbulent; by Manning, hf = n²V²L/R^(4/3), so the loss varies as the square of the velocity.

41. The hydraulic radius is given by _______.
A. Area divided by wetted perimeter
B. Square root of area
C. Wetted perimeter divided by area
D. Area divided by square of the wetted perimeter
Answer: A
Solution: Hydraulic radius R = A/P, the flow area divided by the wetted perimeter.

42. The ratio of the unit weight of the fluid to the unit weight of water at 4 °C is known as ________.

Answer: B
Solution: Specific gravity = γ(fluid)/γ(water at 4 °C), a dimensionless ratio.

57. A cable weighing 0.4 pound per foot and 80 feet long is to be suspended with sag of 80 feet. Determine the maximum tension of the cable.

60. What is the branch of engineering mechanics which refers to the study of rigid body in motion without reference to the force that causes the motion?
A. Statics
B. Kinetics
C. Kinematics
D. Dynamics
Answer: C
Solution: Kinematics describes motion (displacement, velocity, acceleration) without considering the forces that cause it. Kinetics relates forces to motion.

Answer: A
Solution: A couple is two equal, opposite, parallel forces with different lines of action; it produces pure rotation (a moment).

63. What is the concurrent force system?

Solution: In a concurrent force system the lines of action of all forces pass through (act at) the same point.

72. What is the unit of strain?
