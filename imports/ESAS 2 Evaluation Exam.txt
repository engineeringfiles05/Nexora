1. What refers to the state at which liquid and gaseous phases are indistinguishable?
A. Critical Point
B. Triple Point
C. Boiling Point
D. Freezing Point
Answer: A
Solution: At the critical point the saturated liquid and saturated vapour states coincide, so the two phases can no longer be told apart.

2. What is the value of the work done for a closed, reversible, isometric system?
A. Cannot be determined
B. Positive
C. Negative
D. Zero
Answer: D
Solution: Isometric means constant volume. W = ∫p dV = 0 since dV = 0.

3. It is estimated that a certain mine will have an annual output of 30,000 tons of ore, and that the ore body will be exhausted within a period of 20 years. The management cost will be P350,000 annually, and the cost of operating the mine and the smelter plant is p48 per ton. The processed ore and sold for P 17.50 per ton. The sinking fund rate of interest is 8% and the annual dividend rate is to be 15%. Determine the valuation of the property now.
A. P1,165,728.50
B. P1,065,728.48
C. P2,165,728.49
D. P2,065,728.47
Answer: D
Solution: With a selling price of P71.50 per ton: annual net income A = 30,000(71.50 − 48) − 350,000 = P355,000
• Sinking fund factor (8%, 20 yr) = 0.08/[(1.08)²⁰ − 1] = 0.08/3.66096 = 0.021852
• Hoskold valuation: P = A/(dividend rate + SFF) = 355,000/(0.15 + 0.021852) = P2,065,728.47
• Review note: the PDF prints a selling price of P17.50, which is below the P48 operating cost; the choices fit P71.50 per ton.

5. A steam engine operating between a boiler temperature of 220°C and a condenser temperature of 35°C delivers 8 hp. If its efficiency is 30% of that for a Carnot engine operating between these temperature limits, how many calories are exhausted to the considered each seconds?
A. 11,240
B. 14,120
C. 12,140
D. 12,665
Answer: A
Solution: Carnot efficiency = 1 − 308/493 = 0.3753; actual efficiency = 0.30 × 0.3753 = 0.1126
• Output = 8 × 746 = 5,968 W, so heat input = 5,968/0.1126 = 53,010 W
• Heat exhausted = 53,010 − 5,968 = 47,040 W = 47,040/4.186 ≈ 11,240 cal/s

7. How many BTU are needed to isothermally compress a 4 lbm of 240°F air to twice the original pressure?
A. 321.93 BTU
B. 200.93 BTU
C. -132.93 BTU
D. -244.93 BTU
Answer: C
Solution: T = 240 + 459.67 = 699.67 °R; R(air) = 53.34 ft·lbf/lbm·°R = 0.06855 BTU/lbm·°R
• W = mRT ln(p₁/p₂) = 4 × 0.06855 × 699.67 × ln(1/2) = −132.9 BTU
• For an ideal gas at constant temperature Q = W, so about −132.9 BTU (work done on the gas, heat rejected).

9. The gas constant of the mixture is the _________of the gas constants of the components.

Solution: R(mix) = Σ(mᵢRᵢ)/m, the mass-weighted mean of the components' gas constants.

27. A Carnot cycle consists of the following which one is NOT?
A. Isentropic compression
B. Reversible adiabatic expansion
C. Reversible constant heat addition
D. Reversible adiabatic compression
Answer: C

Isentropic compression (A) and reversible adiabatic compression (D) are the same process and are in the cycle, as is B; C is the odd one out.
