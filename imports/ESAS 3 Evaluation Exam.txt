1. If the temperature of a medium is 0°C, what will the temperature be if it is double?
A. 0°C
B. 273°C
C. 524°R
D. 460°R
Answer: B
Solution: Doubling a temperature means doubling its absolute value: 0 °C = 273.15 K
• 2 × 273.15 = 546.3 K = 273 °C

2. Which of the following items below is NOT one of the three basic operations of Boolean algebra?
A. Logical Addition
B. Logical Complementation
C. Logical Subtraction
D. Logical Multiplication
Answer: C
Solution: The three basic Boolean operations are OR (logical addition), AND (logical multiplication) and NOT (complementation). There is no logical subtraction.

3. Which of the following is NOT a noble gas?

10. A molecular diameter of CO is 3.10 x 10⁻⁸ at 300K and pressure of 100 mmHg. What is the mean free path of the gas in cm?
A. 6.86 x 10⁻³
B. 6.86 x 10⁻⁵
C. 2.86 x 10⁻⁴
D. 6.86 x 10⁻⁹

• Nearest choice: 6.86 × 10⁻⁵ cm
