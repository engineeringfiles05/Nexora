## Findings addressed locally

1. **Reviewee cloud queries failed the access rules.** Role-specific Firestore queries now request published questions, public sources, and the reviewee's own results. Load and save errors are visible.
2. **Account data could remain visible after account changes or empty snapshots.** Account state is cleared on authentication changes, late callbacks from a different UID are ignored, and empty snapshots replace old data. Demo storage uses `nexora.demo.v2.*`; legacy mixed `nexora.v1.*` data remains untouched and is not displayed or uploaded.
3. **Demo role switching could replace a signed-in identity.** Signed-in users retain their UID and role; demo identity switching is guarded and disabled in the navigation.
4. **The countdown and target date were hard-coded.** Both displays use `VITE_REE_EXAM_DATE` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), with explicit unconfigured, invalid, upcoming, today, and past states. No official examination date was invented.
5. **The deployed PDF backend was missing.** The public homepage returned 200, while `/api/health` returned Vercel's plain-text 404. Local changes add `api/index.ts`, shared Express routes, and Vercel routing. Serverless extraction uses a streamed request instead of instance-local background jobs. Upload limits, unavailable-backend errors, deadlines, cancellation, and retry stopping have regression coverage.
6. **Practice defaults disagreed and examinations silently contained fewer questions than requested.** Cards and launcher share defaults. A request exceeding the eligible bank stays in the launcher and explains the shortage.
7. **Signed-in admin views used incomplete or sample data.** Admin-only listeners now load audit history and registered users. Reviewees do not query those collections. The roster has loading, error, empty, and explicit demo states.
